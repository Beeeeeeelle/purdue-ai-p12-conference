Why this conference

Alignment over noise.

AI is already reshaping schools. The question is how we align innovation with the goals, relationships, and outcomes that matter most.

The 2026 conference brings the P-12 community together to share practical resources, research, policies, and lessons learned—so new possibilities serve teaching and learning rather than distract from them.

Keynote speaker

Dr. Kui Xie

Dean and Joanne H. Hook Dean’s Chair in Educational Renewal, College of Education & Human Development, University of Missouri

For more than two decades, Dr. Xie’s research has examined how motivation, engagement, and technology shape learning and teaching. His work connects academic discovery to educational practice and asks how emerging technologies—including AI—can be used meaningfully in schools.
